**Example entry**, marked `draft: true` so it never renders in a listing.

Two things worth noticing. `description` is required and bounded at 50–160
characters, so a post cannot ship without a meta description. `category` is one of
five, not fifteen (audit §2.5).

`pillar` is omitted here. A pillar post is its own cluster head and has nothing to
point at; supporting posts reference theirs.
