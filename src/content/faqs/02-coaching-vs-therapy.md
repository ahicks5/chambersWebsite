No, and the difference matters. Therapy treats mental health conditions and
works with your past. Coaching starts from where you are now and works on what
you want to build.

I have been in therapy myself, including EMDR, and it changed my life. If what
you are carrying needs a therapist, I will tell you that on our first call and
help you find one. Some people do both at once, and that works well.
