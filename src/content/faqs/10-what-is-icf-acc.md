The International Coaching Federation is the main credentialing body for
coaching. ACC means I completed accredited training, logged supervised coaching
hours, and passed an exam on their standards and ethics.

Coaching is an unregulated field, so anyone can use the title. The credential is
one way to know the person you are hiring was trained and is accountable to
someone.
