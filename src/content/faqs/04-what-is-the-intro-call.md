It is 30 minutes, and it is free. We talk about what is going on for you. I will
ask what you are hoping changes, and you can ask me anything about how I work.

No pitch, no pressure. If I am not the right fit, I will say so and point you
somewhere better. That happens, and it is a fine outcome for both of us.
