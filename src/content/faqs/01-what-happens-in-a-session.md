order: 1

We talk. You bring what is taking up the most space right now, and we slow it
down together. I ask questions, and we look at what is under the thing you came
in with. You leave with a clearer read on what you are dealing with and a next
step that feels right to you.

Sessions are 60 minutes, by video.
