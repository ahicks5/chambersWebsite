question: What if I am not sure I am burned out enough?
order: 5
page: coaching
reviewed: false
---

You do not need to hit a threshold to get help. Most of the people I work with
are still performing well, which is exactly why nobody around them has noticed.

If you are tired in a way that rest does not fix, or you are doing fine on paper
and it does not feel like it, that is enough of a reason to have a conversation.
