Growing up, I learned early what it meant to feel different. I was regularly
bullied and sat through sermons from my own father about how homosexuality was
the worst kind of sin.

That drive brought me a lot of personal and professional achievement. Eventually,
it left me burned out, disconnected, and painfully out of alignment with the life
I truly wanted.

I stopped living to prove myself worthy and started living proactively in support
of my core values and signature strengths. That shift helped me connect with a
deep desire to more meaningfully help others to do the same.
