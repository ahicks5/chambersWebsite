Short version: this site collects what you type into its two forms, and nothing
else. There is no advertising, no tracking pixels, and nothing is sold or shared
for marketing.

## What gets collected

**The contact form.** Your name, your email address, and your message. It reaches
John by email and is stored by the site's host so he can reply. That is the only
reason it exists.

**The burnout check.** Your email address, so the check can be sent to you and so
you can be added to the mailing list. Every email includes an unsubscribe link,
and unsubscribing removes you.

**Booking a call.** The scheduling calendar on the contact page is provided by
Calendly. When you book, you give your details to Calendly directly, under their
privacy policy rather than this one. The calendar does not load until you scroll
to it.

**Server logs.** Like any website, this one is served by a host that keeps
ordinary request logs, including IP addresses, for a short period. Those are not
used to build a profile of you.

## What does not happen

- No advertising or retargeting pixels.
- No selling, renting or sharing your details for anyone else's marketing.
- No cookies set by this site for tracking. Calendly may set its own when the
  calendar loads.

## Coaching confidentiality

What you say in a coaching session is separate from this website and is covered
by the coaching agreement, not this page. John holds an ACC credential with the
International Coaching Federation and works to their code of ethics, which
includes confidentiality.

If what you are carrying needs a therapist rather than a coach, he will say so
and help you find one. That conversation is confidential too.

## Getting your information removed

Email <johnchambers@coachingwithjc.com> and ask. You do not need a reason, and
you do not need to explain. Form submissions and mailing-list entries are deleted
on request.

## Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the change will be described here
rather than quietly applied.
