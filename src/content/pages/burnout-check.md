Burnout does not announce itself. It creeps in while you are still performing,
still hitting deadlines, still the person everyone relies on. That is exactly why
it goes unnoticed for so long — by everyone, including you.

This is ten yes-or-no statements. It takes about two minutes. You get back a
short, honest read on where you are, and one practice to try this week.

It is not a diagnosis and it does not pretend to be one. If what comes back
suggests you need more than a practice, it says so.
